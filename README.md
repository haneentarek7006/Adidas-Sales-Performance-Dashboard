# 👟 Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive multi-perspective Power BI dashboard analyzing Adidas sales performance, profit margins, product categories, retailers, and sales methods across US regions between 2020 and 2021.

![Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-09-12%20032228.png)

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Performance Highlights
- Total Sales: $89.8M
- Total Profit: $33.16M
- Profit Margin %: 36.92%
- Total Orders: 9,641 orders
- Units Sold: 2M units
- Average Order Value (AOV): $9.32K

---

## 📈 Visualizations & Key Insights

- Sales & Profit Trend Over Time: Line/Area chart displaying revenue expansion from early 2020 to late 2021, highlighting substantial growth starting Q4 2020.
- Sales by Product Category: Men's Street Footwear generated the highest sales ($21M), followed by Women's Apparel ($18M) and Men's Athletic Footwear ($15M).
- Sales by Retailer: West Gear is the leading partner with $24M, followed by Foot Locker ($22M) and Sports Direct ($18M).
- Sales by Sales Method: Channel breakdown showing performance across In-store, Online, and Outlet sales ($35.7M / 39.71% highest channel share).
- Sales by Region: West Region accounts for the highest market share ($27M), followed by the Northeast ($19M) and Southeast ($16M).

---

## 🛠️ Power BI Tools & Techniques Used
- Business Intelligence & Visualization: Microsoft Power BI
- Data Transformation & ETL: Power Query
- Calculations & Measures: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Interactivity: Dynamic Retailer/Channel Slicers, Timeline Date Range Filters, and Cross-filtering Visuals
